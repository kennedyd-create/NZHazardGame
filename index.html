HAZARD RANGER: AOTEAROA

A field-exploration quiz game on New Zealand’s natural hazards

Kia ora! You’ve just joined GNS Science as a trainee Hazard Ranger. Aotearoa New Zealand sits right on the edge of the Pacific "Ring of Fire" — which means earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, cyclones and floods are all part of life here.

Walk through five training zones collecting Hazard Files, then sit the final Hazard Ranger Exam. Answer questions correctly to earn gold coins. Stuck? A hint will cost you a few coins. Get it wrong, and you’ll lose a few too — so think before you answer!

←↑→↓ or WASD — walk
Space / Enter — talk to whoever you’re facing
On touch devices, use the on-screen pad below the map.

Note: this game auto-saves your progress in this browser, on this device — closing the tab and coming back later will pick up right where you left off. Playing on a different device, browser, or in private/incognito mode won’t carry your progress over. Use Restart Training in the top bar any time to wipe your save and begin again.

HOW TO PLAY

←↑→↓ / WASD — walk one tile
Space / Enter — talk to the character you’re facing
Hazard-tape barriers block the exit until everyone required in that zone is solved.
Correct answers earn gold coins and XP, and add a Hazard File to your journal.
Stuck? Hit Hint — it costs a few coins but gives you a clue.
Wrong answers cost a few coins too, so read carefully before choosing.
Characters marked "bonus" are optional — good for extra coins and harder challenge questions.
Watch for THE SIREN — a flashing red figure that sometimes appears in a zone. Answer its question right for a big coin bonus; answer wrong and it takes a big chunk of your gold, even into the negatives. You can walk away without answering if you don’t want to risk it.
Finish all five zones, then find the Examiner at the Hazard Ranger Exam for your final assessment.

RESTART TRAINING?

This clears all coins, XP, and Hazard Files — including your saved progress in this browser — and starts you over at the GNS Field Station. This can’t be undone.

PREVIEW & JUMP TO ZONE

Preview mode leaves every hazard-tape barrier open so you can walk straight to any part of the training ground. Questions still show their real content — nothing is pre-answered.

Jumping to a zone turns preview mode on automatically.
